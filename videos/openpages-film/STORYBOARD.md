## Video direction

Palette and typography come from frame.md. Every reveal follows the 120 BPM score,
with strong changes every 2 seconds. Brand/UI paper grounds alternate with a single
dark SuperCompress sequence. Reveals evolve across the whole scene. Final holds:
6.8–8 in frame 1, 6.4–8 in frame 2, 6.5–8 in frame 3, 5.9–8 in frame 4.
No slideshows, floating decorations, pastel card grids, gratuitous counters or
front-load-and-freeze staging. Story is specific to Space, Pages, citations, MCP.
